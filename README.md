# Human-Resources-in-Digital-Age
 This course explores the importance of data analysis, communication, and planning, essential skills for Human Resources in their new role as strategic partners to business leaders in the digital age. It covers HR metrics, talent management in a changing workplace, the impact of technology, and building a future-proof HR strategy.
 
* Module 1: HR Analytics and Business Partnering
* Module 2: Managing Disruption and The Future of HR
